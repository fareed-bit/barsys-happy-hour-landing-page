Records and health

Review retention dates and place records on hold. Eligible expired, unconverted inquiries and unlinked imported email copies can be removed only after explicit owner confirmation. Removing a dashboard email copy leaves Gmail unchanged and prevents that message from being imported again. Eligible completed events have a separate seven-year retirement review. Email-linked events require coordinated review. Email copies linked to active events and records needed for audits, disputes or insurance must be kept.

Checking database and email queue…

Download removal ledger for recovery — keep this restricted file outside database backups and refresh it after each removal. It contains record identifiers, reviewer details and retained aggregate financial totals; keep it private.

Purchase receipts for expensing

Receipt photos uploaded in the Purchase & pack step are financial records kept for seven years with the event. Export a CSV (event, date, supplier, amount, file link) for a paid-on range; leave both dates blank for every receipt.
