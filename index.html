A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

first cheers.

Have something else in mind?
Let's talk

What's included in my happy hour?

Everything in your selected package is included. Optional upgrades are shown separately with a price when approved, or marked for a custom quote. The standard baseline includes two hours of service, machines, bartending, setup and cleanup. Tax and final scope are confirmed in your proposal.

How many people can we host?

The instant package baseline covers 20 to 250 guests in NYC. Enter your actual headcount for other sizes; we keep your details and flag the plan for a custom quote.

Are mocktails part of the experience?

Yes. Every event includes non-alcoholic options. Select Fluid Code for an alcohol-free collection, or let the Barsys team recommend a mix for your guests.

Where do you host events?

The service is built around New York City office events. Add your location to the plan; the Barsys team will confirm availability and any location-specific requirements. For other locations, get in touch directly.

Can this become a regular office event?

Absolutely. Choose a recurring program in the planner and share your preferred cadence. The team will discuss pricing, scheduling and commitment details. This preview does not automatically apply recurring discounts.

Do you coordinate with our building?

The Barsys team coordinates delivery and setup logistics with your office. Share your building or facilities contact during the planning process so access requirements can be confirmed.

Does a proposal request hold my date?

No. Sending an inquiry does not reserve a date, sign an agreement or take payment. A date is held only after a signed event agreement and receipt of the required deposit, followed by written confirmation from Barsys. Your proposal defines the amount and deadlines.

What should I tell you about building approval or insurance?

Share whether venue permission is confirmed or pending, and whether the building needs a certificate of insurance. Exact access instructions and insurance naming can follow. A request in this form does not confirm coverage or alcohol-service authorization.

Can you guarantee allergen-free drinks?

No. Drinks are prepared in a shared service environment and the absence of allergens cannot be guaranteed. Share event-level ingredient needs with the team before sign-off; avoid entering named guests' medical information.

Are event photos automatically approved for publicity?

No. Choosing a package, creating a plan or expressing marketing interest does not grant permission to publish identifiable guests. Publication and any dedicated photography coverage need their own written approval and scope.
