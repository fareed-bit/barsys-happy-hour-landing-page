A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before the
first cheers.

Have something else in mind?
Let's talk

What's included?

Two hours of open-bar service, Barsys machines, bartenders, spirits, mixers, ice, cocktail and zero-proof options, setup and cleanup. Tax and extras are confirmed in your proposal.

How many guests?

20 to 250 guests in NYC at the listed rates. Other sizes are quoted individually.

Are mocktails included?

Yes. Every event has zero-proof options, or choose an alcohol-free collection.

Where do you host?

New York City offices and venues. Tell us the location and we confirm availability and building access.

Does an inquiry hold my date?

No. A date is held after a signed agreement and deposit.

Can this become a regular event?

Yes. Ask for a recurring program in the planner and we will work out cadence and terms with you.
