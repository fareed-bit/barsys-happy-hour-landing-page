BARSYS 360

The countertop cocktail machine.

Six insulated 25 oz canisters hold 152 oz of spirits, fresh juice and mixers, cold for six hours without refrigeration. Every measure is calibrated to a tenth of an ounce, so the fortieth pour matches the first.

  • No plumbing, no compressor, one outlet
  • Pairs with the Barsys app over Bluetooth 5.0 (iOS and Android)
  • 2,000+ classics, plus menus composed by Barbot, the Barsys AI
  • Any standard glass, centered under the ring
  • 44 × 20 × 43 cm, about 10 lbs

HOW IT POURS

One evening. Four moves.

  • Pair it. Plug in and pair with the free Barsys app.
  • Pick the drink. Guests choose from the event menu on the tablet.
  • Load the bar. Spirits, juice and mixers go into the canisters.
  • It pours. Every measure exact; the bartender garnishes and serves.

SHAKER PRO

The shaker that lights the way.

Pick a recipe in the Barsys AI app, pour until the light says stop, then shake and serve. Accurate to under a milliliter, about 1⁄30 oz.

  • Light-guided pour signals: start, slow, stop
  • Removable tin, balanced weight, USB-C fast charge
  • Bluetooth 5.1, free Barsys AI app on iOS and Android
  • 1,965 crafted recipes, plus any drink Barbot builds on demand
  • Tin rinses like a classic shaker; the electronics stay dry

AT YOUR EVENT

Machines pour. People host.

A Barsys happy hour pairs 360s on the bar with Shaker Pro stations for the bartenders. Guests pick a drink on the tablet, the 360 pours it exactly, and the team shakes, garnishes and hands it over. Zero-proof menus run on the same machines.

The Barsys team brings, sets up and takes down everything: machines, tablets, ingredients, ice and glassware as agreed in your proposal.
