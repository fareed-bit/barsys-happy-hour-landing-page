# Privacy requests and retention operations

Operational owner: fareed@barsys.com. This procedure is not a promise of automatic deletion or a substitute for applicable obligations.

1. Record receipt, requested scope, due date under the applicable process, assigned reviewer and status in a restricted case record. Verify identity proportionately using the existing contact channel; do not ask for unnecessary identity documents.
2. Locate inquiry, proposal/acceptance, preparation, operations, inventory references, imported email and relevant exports. Search only the records needed for the request. Do not export unrelated clients.
3. Check active service, financial, dispute, insurance and other preservation obligations. Record the decision and any hold with minimal sensitive text. The seven-year core-record period is a review default, not permission to delete required evidence.
4. For access/correction, assemble only the verified person's applicable records and review third-party data before delivery. Obtain explicit authorization before sending messages from this assistant.
5. For removal, follow RETENTION-OPERATIONS.md. Separate owner controls cover eligible unconverted inquiries, unlinked imported copies and eligible seven-year completed-event retirement. Email-linked events and outside copies still require coordinated review; do not bypass their safeguards.
6. Document what was changed, what remains and why, the reviewer and completion date. Include original Gmail, sent messages and exports in the copy review. Never tell the requester that database removal erases every copy.
7. Preserve a protected disposal ledger separately from any restored database. Restore only into isolation, reconcile removals before serving traffic and test that removed content is not restored to users. If reconciliation is unresolved, keep that restored environment offline.
8. Fareed reviews due inquiries monthly, imported-copy retention monthly and completed-event review dates annually. These are operating responsibilities, not a scheduler already implemented by this application.
